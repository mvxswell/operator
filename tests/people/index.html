People & Delegation Skill Test

Practice decisions about delegation, hiring, training, and accountability. The cases ask you to match responsibility with authority and to distinguish a person's capability gap from a problem in how the work is designed.

Start this test

What this set tests

  • Choose what to delegate and which decisions need boundaries.
  • Match a role or hire to the actual operating need.
  • Respond to performance problems with specific evidence and support.

How to approach the scenarios

  1. Identify who owns the outcome and what they can actually control.
  2. Check whether information, authority, and capacity match the assignment.
  3. Choose a clear next decision rather than a broad personality judgment.

A sample from the question bank

You bill $250 an hour and have a waiting list of clients. You currently spend six hours a week scanning and filing documents. An assistant would do it for $22 an hour.

Feedback and progress

Difficulty adapts as you answer. The final score combines accuracy, difficulty handled, and decision speed; accuracy carries the most weight. Read the explanation after each call, then use the result to choose what to train next. A personal best is stored locally, so compare runs in the same browser to follow your practice.

These are practice scenarios, not a professional certification or a validated hiring assessment. Use the feedback to examine your reasoning. The information needed to answer is in the case; real operating decisions may require facts and advice beyond a short exercise.
