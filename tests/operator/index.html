Operator Test: Business Judgment Assessment

The Operator Test combines decisions from all eight skill areas into one adaptive run. You read a business situation, compare four possible actions, and choose the best supported call using the information in the scenario.

Start this test

What this set tests

  • Find a bottleneck before spending money or adding people.
  • Compare cash, margin, and the opportunity cost of growth.
  • Make tradeoffs in hiring, delegation, sales, and strategy.

How to approach the scenarios

  1. Read the objective and identify the constraint before comparing actions.
  2. Use the numbers and facts supplied; do not invent missing customer demand.
  3. Review the explanation after each answer and use the final breakdown to pick a skill to train.

Feedback and progress

Difficulty adapts as you answer. The final score combines accuracy, difficulty handled, and decision speed; accuracy carries the most weight. Read the explanation after each call, then use the result to choose what to train next. A personal best is stored locally, so compare runs in the same browser to follow your practice.

These are practice scenarios, not a professional certification or a validated hiring assessment. Use the feedback to examine your reasoning. The information needed to answer is in the case; real operating decisions may require facts and advice beyond a short exercise.
