Sales & Marketing Skill Test

Practice diagnosing where a sales and marketing funnel is breaking. The cases ask whether the problem is traffic, qualification, appointments, conversion, or the offer before you spend more to generate activity.

Start this test

What this set tests

  • Find the funnel stage that limits acquired customers.
  • Compare acquisition spending with conversion and contribution.
  • Choose a response to a pricing or sales-process obstacle.

How to approach the scenarios

  1. Follow the counts through the funnel using consistent definitions.
  2. Separate a shortage of opportunities from poor conversion of existing demand.
  3. Choose the intervention nearest the evidence, then consider its cost.

Feedback and progress

Difficulty adapts as you answer. The final score combines accuracy, difficulty handled, and decision speed; accuracy carries the most weight. Read the explanation after each call, then use the result to choose what to train next. A personal best is stored locally, so compare runs in the same browser to follow your practice.

These are practice scenarios, not a professional certification or a validated hiring assessment. Use the feedback to examine your reasoning. The information needed to answer is in the case; real operating decisions may require facts and advice beyond a short exercise.
