Daily Decision: A Business Scenario to Practice Each Day

Daily Decision gives you one dated scenario to work through without a full test session. Choose from four actions, read why one is best supported, and keep a practice streak in this browser. The daily prompt changes with the calendar date.

Try today's decision

What this set tests

  • Apply a decision framework to a concrete operating situation.
  • Notice which facts separate the strongest option from a tempting alternative.
  • Build a repeat practice habit without committing to a long assessment.

How to approach the scenarios

  1. Read the whole scenario and choose before opening the explanation.
  2. Compare your reasoning with the feedback rather than only checking correctness.
  3. Return on the next local calendar day, or use a skill test for more practice now.

A sample from the question bank

Your taproom is full every weekend and distribution accounts are asking for more kegs than you can supply. Fermentation tanks run at 100% and a new tank takes four months to install. A distributor offers to double your account count starting next month.

What should you do?

The test draws from this scenario set. The first question in your run may differ from this sample.

Feedback and progress

After you answer, the explanation shows which action is best supported by the scenario. Your completed day and streak are stored in this browser. Return on the next local calendar day for the next dated prompt, or take a focused skill test when you want a longer practice session.

These are practice scenarios, not a professional certification or a validated hiring assessment. Use the feedback to examine your reasoning. The information needed to answer is in the case; real operating decisions may require facts and advice beyond a short exercise.
