Quick Decisions: 60-Second Business Judgment Quiz

Quick Decisions is a short practice round for making clear business calls under time pressure. Each card offers two choices. Correct answers move you forward; mistakes cost time, so fast guessing is a poor substitute for accurate judgment.

Start this test

What this set tests

  • Recognize a basic constraint without a long analysis.
  • Choose between two plausible operating actions.
  • Balance decision speed with accuracy rather than maximizing clicks.

How to approach the scenarios

  1. Read both choices before answering with a tap, A/D, or the arrow keys.
  2. Look for the action supported by the stated business facts.
  3. Review missed calls afterward and compare accuracy as well as DPM on another round.

A sample from the question bank

Feedback and progress

Your result shows decisions per minute, accuracy, response time, and streak information. Accuracy affects the score, so guessing quickly can reduce the result. Review missed scenarios and try another round to practice the distinction you missed. A short round is a practice session, not a substitute for a full assessment.

These are practice scenarios, not a professional certification or a validated hiring assessment. Use the feedback to examine your reasoning. The information needed to answer is in the case; real operating decisions may require facts and advice beyond a short exercise.
