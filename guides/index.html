Guides

Decisions owners actually have to make

Short, practical guides on people, money, constraints, and where the next hour or dollar should go. Each one ends in the skill test that practices the same call.

  • People & Delegation · 6 min

    What to Do When Your Best Employee Quits

    Do not negotiate in the doorway. In the first 48 hours, protect the work: write down what only they hold, name a temporary owner for each piece, and book a real conversation for the next day.

  • People & Delegation · 5 min

    How to Delegate as a Business Owner

    Owners hoard tasks because the work feels faster in their own hands and because a bad handoff is public. Start with frequent, low-risk work you already understand, and give away the outcome with a clear limit instead of a running commentary.

  • Resource Allocation · 5 min

    When to Hire Your First Employee

    Hire when the business is refusing revenue or your week is full of repeat work someone else could own. Price the fully loaded cost against what those hours are worth, and hire the constraint, not the comfort task.

  • Financial Judgment · 5 min

    How to Price a Service Business

    Price from the hours you can actually sell and the pay the business has to cover, then check that number against what buyers pay and what the job is worth to them. If nearly everyone says yes, the price is a discount.

  • Financial Judgment · 5 min

    How to Improve Cash Flow in a Small Business

    Profit and cash are different clocks. Collect faster, take deposits on work that ties up materials, and time what you owe. Build a 13-week view of cash in and out before you cut anything that brings the next dollar in.

  • Bottleneck Detection · 5 min

    How to Find the Bottleneck in Your Business

    The bottleneck is the step where work piles up and waits, not the person who looks busiest. Measure how much each step finishes and how long work sits in front of it. Make that step better before you add people or tools anywhere else.

  • Bottleneck Detection · 5 min

    Why Is My Business Not Growing

    A business stops growing at a specific step, not everywhere at once. Walk demand, conversion, capacity, delivery, retention, and your own calendar. Put one number on each branch, and work the branch that is actually stuck.

  • Prioritization · 5 min

    How to Prioritize as a Business Owner

    Separate what is loud from what moves revenue, margin, cash, or risk. Rank the real items by impact, then by how much effort they take. When you add something, name what you will stop. Do that review once a week.

  • Sales & Marketing · 5 min

    Why Are Sales Down

    Break sales into traffic, conversion, order value, and purchase frequency. Compare each term with the period before the drop. Fix the term that moved. A story from one customer is a clue, not a diagnosis.

  • Sales & Marketing · 6 min

    How to Get More Customers for a Small Business

    Pick a channel that fits how your buyers already search, ask, or get referred. Wake up past customers and ask for referrals before you fund a new channel. Test one channel at a time, and keep it only if the customers are worth more than they cost.

  • Operations · 5 min

    How to Create SOPs and Systemize Your Business

    Systemize the work that pulls you back in most often. Record yourself doing it, turn that into a short page, and have someone else run the page while you watch. Assign an owner and a review date, or the document becomes folklore.

  • Operations · 5 min

    How to Scale Operations Without Losing Quality

    Growth keeps its quality when the work is the same on purpose, someone checks it at each handoff, and you watch rework and lateness before customers complain. Automate only after the steps stop changing. More volume through a messy step makes a bigger mess.

  • Strategy · 5 min

    Should I Raise Prices

    Raise prices when your costs moved, you are turning work away, or almost every qualified buyer says yes. Announce the change with a date, decide who keeps the old price and for how long, and watch close rate and margin rather than the loudest complaint.

  • Strategy · 5 min

    How to Decide Whether to Expand to a New Location or Market

    Expand when the current business is repeatable without you on site, someone else can run a day, and you can fund a slow start. Test a slice of the new place before you sign a long lease. Write the results that would make you stop.

  • Resource Allocation · 5 min

    How to Allocate a Limited Budget

    Compare the real options on return, how fast cash comes back, and what happens if you are wrong. Fund the constraint first. Money already spent is not a reason to spend more. Keep a buffer so one bet cannot stop payroll.
