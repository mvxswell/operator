How to read a business situation

Every scenario contains the facts you need. The work is to find the constraint, ignore the noise, and choose the move that changes the outcome.

Strong operators do not collect more information by default. They ask which number, if it moved, would change the decision.

What to look for

  • The step that is already at capacity
  • The cost of waiting versus the cost of a wrong hire
  • Whether the next dollar belongs in sales or in delivery

When two options both sound reasonable, prefer the one that removes the bottleneck rather than the one that adds activity around it.

A short sequence

  1. Name the constraint in one sentence.
  2. Reject options that do not move it.
  3. Commit, then read why that call was best.
